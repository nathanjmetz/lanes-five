      How to play

      • Walk: tap the floor.
      • Do things: tap furniture, people or pets to see what you can do.
      • Your menu: tap yourself for walks, parties, birthdays and more.
      • Buy and Build: buttons on the right. Change walls, floors, rooms and furniture.
      • Camera: turn with the arrow buttons, zoom with + and −, or drag with two fingers.
      • Time: the buttons by the clock pause or speed up the day.
      • When you leave: your person keeps living gently at home. Nothing bad happens.
